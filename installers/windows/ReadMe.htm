What you installed

Ghillie (version {#GuiVersion}) lets you send and receive CAMO privately: every payment uses ring signatures (ring size 16), stealth addresses and hidden amounts.

Also included is camod, the CAMO node. Ghillie starts it for you and downloads the CAMO blockchain to your computer, so you don't depend on anyone else's server.

Ghillie never adds a fee of its own. You only pay the normal network fee.

First start

  1. Start Ghillie from the Start menu or the desktop icon.
  2. Create a new wallet, or restore one from your 25 recovery words.
  3. Write your recovery words on paper and keep them safe. Anyone with these words can take your CAMO. Nobody from CAMO will ever ask you for them.
  4. Wait until "Daemon is synchronized" and "Wallet is synchronized" are shown before you send or receive.

Where things are

  • Wallets: Documents\Ghillie\wallets
  • Blockchain: the folder you chose during installation (default C:\ProgramData\camo)
  • Troubleshooting shortcuts: Start menu, Ghillie > Utilities
